# Curriculum a0 b2

**Draft; human pedagogical and Ukrainian translation review required.** Packaged JSON contains five courses fleng-a0 through fleng-b2, 50 lessons (ten/course), 500 vocabulary entries/course dictionary rows and 400 single-choice exercises. These are draft content, not published production courses or complete CEFR assessment coverage.

Each lesson has two goals, grammar focus, theory, examples/context, ten words, five practice choices and three checks. Prerequisites form a cross-level chain but do not lock access. Ukrainian translations are present; individual word examples may be empty because contextual examples occur in theory.

UUIDv5 identities derive from source version/kind/slug or course+normalized word. Legacy catalog remains unchanged by this pipeline. Standard player/grading is reused; no separate speaking/listening/exam engine was added.

The retained [complete curriculum specification](../curriculum-a0-b2.md) lists all 50 titles/goals/topics. [Quality report](../curriculum-content-quality.md) preserves editorial limitations. A0 is internal introductory terminology, not official CEFR certification. Structural validation and SHA acknowledgement do not establish linguistic approval.
